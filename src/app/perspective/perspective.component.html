The Golden Age of Software Development

While the AI wave has undeniably dealt a heavy blow to the traditional job market, it is simultaneously the golden age of software development.

Unshackled from syntax and frameworks, developers are no longer limited by language barriers. We can instantly work with any technology stack and turn roughly 95% of our ideas into reality, shifting our focus entirely to architecture, system structure, and solutions. Coding has become an intensely high-yield and high-intensity source of pure joy.

Why We Still Need to Learn

In this new paradigm, professional software engineering education is actually more meaningful than ever. Knowing how to design clean, scalable project structures and choose the right solutions is what separates professional development from casual vibe coding.

Consequently, years of experience spent merely memorizing syntax and language proficiency have cheapened, offering diminishing returns compared to structural and architectural mastery.

What I Learnt in Sheridan

My education at Sheridan provided me with solid software development fundamentals, core networking concepts, and a complete understanding of the end-to-end Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC). Beyond theoretical knowledge, it shaped my engineering mindset on what makes a software project truly great—focusing on clean, scalable codebases, thoughtful system architecture, and robust design principles.
